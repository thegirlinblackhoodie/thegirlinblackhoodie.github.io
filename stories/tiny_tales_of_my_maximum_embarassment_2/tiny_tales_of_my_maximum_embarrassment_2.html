1. The Pineapple That Was Never Meant to Be


I bought a pineapple bromeliad with a 3.5-inch green pineapple on it. Google said, “In six months, you’ll have a real fruit.” I believed Google. I nurtured it. Loved it. Hyped it up daily.


Six months later: same size. Same audacity.


That’s when ChatGPT gently informed me it was an ornamental plant and I’d be lucky if it tasted remotely sweet. Turns out I wasn’t growing fruit—I was growing false hope.



2. User Error


I bought the new AirPods Pro 3—elite noise cancellation, incredible sound, the whole promise of instant inner peace. Except they kept falling out of my ears.


I adjusted them. Changed the tips. Switched to smaller ones. Tried again. Failed again. Eventually, defeated and slightly offended, I went back to my old AirPods, blaming poor design, bad luck, and possibly my ears themselves.


A few days later, it hit me. I had been wearing the left AirPod in my right ear and the right one in my left ear the entire time. The technology was flawless. The user, unfortunately, was not.



3. Six Months Too Early


My sister is having a baby, and my only responsibility was to buy cute baby things. She specifically said, “Get cute baby stuff.”


I took this job very seriously. Too seriously.


A few days later, reality caught up with me: she won’t be coming back until the baby is six months old. Every single thing I bought was newborn size.


Congratulations to me for wasting money on clothes the baby will outgrow before I even see them.



4. That's my SPOT!


I moved to a new place and have a parking spot. I don’t have a car. Two neighbors asked if they could use it. One even invited me over and fed me home-cooked food. I said they could share.


They did not share. They fought. They involved me. They escalated.


So I did the only rational thing left: I placed a plant in the middle of my parking spot to assert dominance. Very Sheldon Cooper. Except in my case, it actually was my spot. The plant now stands there silently, like a leafy bouncer enforcing my authority.
