I usually let my phone go down to 1% or even let it die before I charge it. But this time was different. I woke up at 4 a.m., saw it had 1%, and plugged it in. It didn't show the charging sign. I waited, but after a few seconds, it just shut down.

It had happened before, so I left it plugged in for a while. After some time, I tried again — still nothing. Just a black screen with that tiny red battery icon.


I googled and found out I could try a "force restart" — press volume up, volume down, and power together. Basically CPR for my phone. It didn't work. I kept trying like a desperate doctor until even that red battery sign disappeared.


It felt like one of those Bollywood hospital scenes — the beeping slows down, the doctors shout "don't give up!", then… flatline. I was still hoping for that cringe moment where the hero suddenly opens his eyes. Comes back to life after being dead for a while— but nothing.


I didn't want to give up, so I left it charging all night like it was on life support. When I woke up, still dead. I tried one last time in the morning — and somehow, it worked! It came back to life.


Guess I did get my Bollywood moment — just not the romantic kind where my dress gets stuck in someone's watch. Mine was the full-on coma, CPR, and plot-twist revival.
