This month has been hectic at work.


With multiple launches planned and a lot going on, my organization decided to boost morale by hosting Snack Attacks every Tuesday, Wednesday, and Thursday around 3 p.m.


There were cookies one day, corn dogs another, then deep-fried mozzarella sticks, donuts, waffles... I happened to be in the office for all of those. Not that I was a huge fan of any of them, but I still grabbed a snack and hung out with my colleagues.


Then came the one day I decided to work from home. They served adorable little cakes in jars. Of course. I missed them. I told the organizer how much I regretted not coming to the office that day.


The following week brought pretzels, churros, and chicken wings. I was in the office for every single one. Then I worked from home again. That day they had chocolate cherry cake, cheesecake, and pie. Seriously?


I told the organizer, "Why do you always have cake on the days I don't come to the office?"


By this point, everyone had started noticing the pattern. It had become an office joke.


Then came mochi donuts, potstickers, and bao. I was there. No cake.


The next week? Ube cheesecake. The one day I wasn't in the office. Again.


This time, I gave the organizer a stern warning, "We're going to have serious problems."


At this point, my relationship with cake had become meme-worthy.


Finally... one day when I was actually in the office... they served panna cotta. Not exactly cake. But definitely a close relative.


And this time, the entire office was determined that I wouldn't miss it. The organizer tagged me. My VP tagged me too. People messaged me to stop whatever I was doing and go grab my dessert before it disappeared.


After weeks of losing every battle... me and dessert finally had our happy ending.
