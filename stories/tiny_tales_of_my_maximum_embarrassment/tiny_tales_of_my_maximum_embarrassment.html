1. The Sunrise That Never Was


We sat on the wrong side of Santa Monica beach at 4 a.m., waiting for a sunrise that would never come. Somewhere behind us, the real sun did a full SRK-style “Palat(turn around)… Palat(turn around)…”, but we were too committed to our wrong life choices to turn around.

2. The Fork Flip Stunt


At an internship dinner, I tried twirling noodles. Noodles resisted. Fork rebelled. It flipped out of my hand like I was auditioning for a stunt show. The whole table watched my career leave my body.

3. The Six-Inch Heels


Ordered six-inch heels for graduation. A person who has never worn heels in her life decided to start at the Olympics level. Took one step inside college… immediately fell. Ceremony hadn't even begun; my humiliation had.

4. The Lassi Massacre


Went to lunch with a colleague, got free lassi, felt blessed. Balanced it on top of my lunch like a circus act. Dropped it once—no spill. Dropped it twice—still safe. Third time? It exploded. All over him. His black sweater turned strawberry pink.

5. The Halloween DTSJ


We went to Downtown San Jose for Halloween—normally the busiest place ever. But apparently, not for us. We ended up being the only ones there: ten people in full costume starring in our own accidental horror-comedy film.

6. The Holi Walk of Shame


Played Holi at work, colleague vanished right after. I re-entered the office drenched in colors, looking like a limited-edition Pride Month mascot. The silence in the hallway was louder than the festival.
