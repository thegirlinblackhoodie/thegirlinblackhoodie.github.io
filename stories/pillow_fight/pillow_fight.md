# The Day My Fight-or-Flight Picked the Wrong Option

I was working from home, minding my own business, vibing in the comfort of my room, when suddenly I heard a voice — a man's voice. Which was weird… because my flatmate had left in the morning.

Meaning: the house was supposed to be empty.
Meaning: this was not part of my work-from-home plan.

My door was closed but not locked. So naturally, I tiptoed across the room and shut it quietly. I held my breath and listened. Another noise. My paranoia said: This is it. Today’s the day you go viral in a crime documentary.

For reasons still unknown to science, I decided to open the door and confront whoever was outside. Fight-or-flight? I chose “fight,” apparently. A brave move for someone whose entire upper-body strength comes from carrying grocery bags twice a month.

I swung the door open dramatically.... and discovered I was holding a pillow. 

A PILLOW!!! Not a bat. Not pepper spray. Not even a water bottle. A pillow — the weapon of toddlers at sleepovers.

My soul left my body. What exactly was my plan? Smother the criminal with comfort?

At that point, I accepted my fate. I dropped the pillow slightly, lowered my head, and mentally prepared to surrender. 

Then I looked up... It was the maintenance guy. Doing his job and fixing the kitchen sink issue we had raised few days back. Probably wondering why a stranger was challenging him to a duel with pillow.

I put the pillow down, my dignity down even further, and walked back to my room like nothing happened.
