I decided to be healthy and productive for one evening. Dangerous combination already.


I went on a walk. Just me, my thoughts, my airpods, my keys. After 30 minutes of walking, I entered ✨Smart & Final✨. I carefully selected groceries that would sustain me through difficult times: one Starbucks vanilla latte bottle, one milk can, one lemonade bottle.


That’s it. Not groceries. Just beverages for different emotional states.


I stood in line for 15 minutes. FIFTEEN. Finally my turn came. The cashier scanned everything while I casually looked at my phone in my hand… and realized it was my work phone. No Apple Pay. No cards. No cash.


I checked my pockets anyway because denial is free. Airpods case. Keys. Nothing else. I had walked into a grocery store with the financial preparedness of a Victorian orphan.


I immediately panicked and told the cashier to stop. I said I would put everything back myself because somehow I believed this would restore my dignity. The cashier politely said she appreciated that. Which honestly made it worse because now she thought I was a good person experiencing hard times.


So I carried my three emotional support beverages back to their shelves. Then I walked home in shame.


I still needed milk. So I ordered on DoorDash. I also decided to order Pistachio Ice cream to cheer myself up. I started watching TV. I opened the app after sometime to check where the guy was only to see it was marked Delivered!


Not to me, obviously. The photo showed apartment 4. Not MY apartment 4. Like I was in a gamified mode of DoorDash and I had a side quest to complete to get my order. It was 11:30 PM. Now I’m outside in pajamas playing a scavenger hunt for milk which I don't remember signing up for.


I started narrowing down possibilities like Sherlock Holmes with anxiety:


“It's highly unlikely to be confused with buildings on the opposite side of the road. So it should be the apartments to the immediate left or right. That reduces search space from 5 buildings to 2. Now DoorDash drivers have got confused before with the one on the right…so now it's just 1 building that I need to search. Well this is not that bad....”


So I entered the neighboring apartment complex. At this point if anyone had seen me wandering around at midnight, I would’ve had absolutely no convincing explanation.


I found apartment 4. And there it was. My little DoorDash bag was sitting outside the door like abandoned treasure. I grabbed it and walked away very quickly.


Back home, I opened the vanilla latte bottle and saw weird white pieces floating inside. Fantastic. After everything I had been through that day, the least the latte could do was to push through and survive for a few mins outside the fridge for me!


I stared at it for a full minute. Then I threw it away because I was not about to survive all this only to get food poisoning. The milk also looked questionable but decided it was a disappointment to handle for another day.


I tried eating pistachio ice cream afterward to recover mentally, but it tasted less like pistachio and more like frozen sugar with green food color.


Overall, the entire night felt like the universe watched me attempt basic adult tasks and said: “Oh sweetie… I can’t watch this anymore. Please just stop. We’ll try again tomorrow. ❤️”
