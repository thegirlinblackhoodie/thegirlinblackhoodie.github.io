I have real friends.

Yes, I do.


Within a few months of moving to the Bay Area...


I fell sick. I had severe chest pain. I knew it was my bones hurting. Given my medical history, I was shit scared.


I went to urgent care and was told I had a rib cage fracture. Later, the reports said it wasn't. The hospital staff messed with me and refused to hand over my X-rays so I could show them to my doctor in India. They made me run between Sunnyvale, Mountain View, and San Jose the entire day.


When I called my friend, he said,

"Okay, I am at work. Can't talk now. Bye."


My friend was just preparing me to be a strong and independent woman.



A year later, I ended up in the ER...


I got tonsillitis. I went to urgent care, but the doctors overreacted and told me my windpipe could close completely within an hour. They told me that if it happened, I should call 911 and go to the ER immediately.


At the ER, they said they were going to give me a steroid shot. I was scared, so I called my friends.


They said,

"Yeah... why are you calling us? Do what they say."


Again, just making me strong and independent.

They always cared for me so much.



My friends have a favorite Indian restaurant...


They like it because, for $25, they get an unlimited thali.


The first time they took me there, I puked after reaching home.

The second time, the same thing happened.

The third time, I refused to go. They insisted I had only puked because I ate too much since it was unlimited. So I deliberately ate very little.

I still puked after reaching home.


A few days later, they wanted to go to the same restaurant again, even though they knew it made me sick. I sat there without eating anything.

The next time they made the same plan, I decided I would rather stay home.


Don't get them wrong.

I was putting on weight.

They cared about my health.

This was probably their way of making sure I stopped eating outside food altogether.



I have breathing issues...


Hiking has always been difficult for me because of that.


One day my friends told me it was a 3-mile hike.

Halfway through, I realized it was actually an 11-mile hike.

I told them I couldn't continue and walked back to the car.


We were in the middle of nowhere.

I sat alone in the parking lot from 3 p.m. until 9 p.m., when it was dark.

Alone.

Scared.

With no way to get back home.


Again, just making me strong and fearless.

So considerate.



My friends planned a road trip to San Diego and Mexico...


They asked me to join one week before the trip.

Because someone else had dropped out.

There was space in the car.

And they needed another person to split the bills.


I was the first person they thought of when they needed someone to fill the empty seat.

I am honored.

Truly.



My friends booked tickets for the Coldplay concert...


Four seats were together.

Two were far away.

One guy wasn't even interested in coming and was already trying to sell his ticket on Ticketmaster.


So naturally, they decided the two separate seats would be for him and me.


I was single, and they only hoped that whoever bought his ticket would be a nice guy so I'd have my meet-cute moment.


Such amazing friends.

Most of them were single themselves, yet they were willing to sacrifice their own chances just so I could have mine.



One day before Ganesh Chaturthi...


My friends asked me to come early to help.

I took the Caltrain all the way there.

I was exhausted.


The first thing they said was,

"Oh lol, you look so drained. Cool... come cut the veggies."


I spent the whole day helping them cook.

When the guests started arriving, they went to get dressed and told me I could go immediately after.


Finally, when it was my turn, they called me back.

In front of all the guests.

"Can you go wash all the utensils in the sink and clean the kitchen? The guests have started coming in, and it won't look nice."


They considered me so close that they treated me like their maid that day.

It's just that we're super close friends.

Nothing else.

I feel blessed.



My birthday...


None of my friends wished me on my birthday.

Nope.


It wasn't that they forgot.

They just wanted me to have my space.

They didn't want to bother me.

They wanted me to have peace.

They wanted me to have my solitude.


In return for everything my friends have done for me, so much care, love, and consideration that they have shown me over the years, I only felt it was right to return the same favor to them by separating myself from them.


It was too much of a sacrifice they had made for me.

Too much effort they had put into maintaining a friendship with me.


I only felt it was right to free them from all that burden.

And set them free.
