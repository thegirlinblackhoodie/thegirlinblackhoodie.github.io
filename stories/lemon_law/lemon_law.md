# The Day I Used Barney’s Lemon Law for Real

I was turning 29 and realized I didn't have a boyfriend. I was nowhere close to having one either. So I decided to take control — made a dating app profile and started swiping till I hit the limit.

One guy seemed nice. We spoke briefly and decided to meet the next day. It felt fast, but I said yes. We met at Starbucks.

He looked at me and said, “You look better in person. You should update your profile.” I thought that was sweet — until my friend later told me it was a backhanded compliment. Still, I chose to be happy about it. I don’t even remember when I last got a compliment… or if I ever did.

There wasn’t even a real conversation. He went straight into talking about sex — asking my body count, if I’d ever had a threesome, or if I’d ever been curious about girls. It was weird, awkward, and honestly a little scary. It wasn't the kind of conversation I would have liked to have on my 1st date, not 2nd, not 10th, actually NEVER!

5 mins into the date, I said I had work and needed to leave — applying the Lemon Law I learned from How I Met Your Mother. He offered to drop me home. I said no. He said, “Don’t be scared. I’m not a bad guy.” That line made my skin crawl.

When I got home, I opened the app to block him, and guess what! He’d already blocked me! 

The audacity — he made me uncomfortable, but he got the satisfaction of ending it first...
