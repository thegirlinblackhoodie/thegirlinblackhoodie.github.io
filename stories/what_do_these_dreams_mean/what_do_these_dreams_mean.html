I went to bed at 10 PM, expecting a normal night of sleep. Instead, my brain apparently had other plans.


I woke up at 2:30 AM after a nightmare. Eventually I fell asleep again, only to wake up at 4:30 AM because of another one. Neither dream made much sense, but both managed to be weirdly intense.


Dream #1:


I'm in my old house in Bangalore, standing in the living room.


I open a window and see a white lion outside. Naturally, I'm terrified. The lion then starts talking to me. In perfect English.


Instead of doing something sensible—like closing the window, locking everything, and calling for help—I stand there listening while it calmly explains that it means no harm and would really appreciate being let inside.


For some reason, I decide to walk over to another window on the same wall, maybe three feet away. I opened it. There is another lion. This one is much less interested in conversation and much more interested in eating me. It immediately tries to pounce. I back away and head toward a door on the side of the room.


Now, at this point, any reasonable person would stop opening things. Apparently Dream Me is not a reasonable person. I open the door. There is a third lion. It immediately launches itself at me. And that's when I wake up.


Dream #2:


In the second dream, I've just visited a temple with a very close friend. We're walking back across a bridge.


My friend is carrying a ridiculous amount of stuff stacked in his hands, to the point where he can barely see where he's going. Then, somehow, he steps onto a roller skate.


Just one. He slips, slides, and falls off the bridge into the water below.


Neither of us knows how to swim. Some parts of the dream are blurry now, and I don't remember whether anyone tried to help. What I do remember is that he eventually drowns.


The whole thing feels horribly sudden. One second we're walking back from a temple, and the next I'm trying to process the fact that someone just died because of a roller skate.


I spend the rest of the dream wandering around, shocked and sad, not really knowing what to do. Eventually I get home and tell my friends what happened.


They don't seem to care. And while I already know these friends don't really give 2 fucks about me, I still expected at least a little concern for the fact that someone had literally died. Nothing.


I end up sitting alone in my room, feeling sad, scared, and strangely abandoned. That's when I wake up.


What Does Any of This Mean?


I have absolutely no idea. A talking white lion trying to negotiate its way into my house. Two additional lions whose communication style is significantly more direct. A fatal roller-skate incident. Friends, who are probably not worth calling friends? My subconscious clearly had a very busy night.


If anyone has a theory about what these dreams mean, feel free to share it. I'd genuinely love to hear other interpretations. Because right now, all I know is that I went to sleep at 10 PM and somehow ended up starring in two completely different psychological thrillers before sunrise.
