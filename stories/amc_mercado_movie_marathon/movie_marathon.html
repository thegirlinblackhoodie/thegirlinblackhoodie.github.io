My dad told me Dhurandhar 2 was released. I had watched the first movie in theatres and had liked it, so I thought of watching the second one too. I opened the AMC Mercado website and saw there was a 10:45 AM show on Saturday morning. The theatre was almost empty, giving me the option to select the best seat for the movie.


Now, in hindsight, an almost empty theatre on a Saturday morning should have raised at least some suspicion. But at that moment I chose to ignore it. Super excited, I booked tickets for it.


I knew the movie was 4 hours long, so I bought some food and drinks before entering the auditorium. I sat there wondering why on a Saturday the screening of this movie had such an empty theatre. Other than me, there were at max 2 more people.


I texted my friends saying how lucky I was to not only get the best seat for this movie but also an almost empty theatre. Super excited, I opened the AMC Mercado website again to see what other movies were playing that day. I wanted to select a movie such that it would align perfectly with the ending of the current movie.


So I started my math. Given the movie time was 10:45 AM, plus around 20 minutes of advertisements that AMC runs, and then almost 4 hours of movie. In the US, you get no break or intermission no matter how long the movie is. So that would mean the movie would end around 3:05 PM.


Based on that I looked for movies and saw there was Anaconda at 4:00 PM. I had watched the trailer some time back and had liked it. I also saw the theatre for that movie was barely filled, so I thought maybe I was having the best AMC day and I should make complete use of it.


So I booked the next movie. The current one hadn’t even started, but I was already on high energy and happiness. I had successfully planned a full movie marathon for my Saturday. And I was sure I was going to have a great time.


Then eventually the movie started. And the first scene felt familiar. Like… very familiar. I was like — did the first movie begin in a similar way? I was confused. I didn’t want to believe it. Then I thought maybe this was a recap before starting part 2? But there was no indication of that.


Then the second scene came. Also familiar. At this point the denial phase was ending and acceptance was slowly beginning.


It was the first movie. AMC had just decided to re-release it. Which clearly explains the empty theatre. Now I had no option other than sitting through it because I already had another movie booked for when this one would end, and I needed to be here.


That’s when another realization hit me.


The first movie was only 3 hours 30 minutes long. This meant the movie would end around 2:30 PM… and I had to find a way to kill time from then till 4:00 PM.


At this point I accepted my fate.


I entered AMC Mercado at 10:45AM and left at 6:30PM. I had spent 7 hours and 45 minutes in the theatre on a Saturday.


Yes, I, in my full consciousness, had planned a full Saturday movie marathon at AMC Mercado. The only small problem was… I had picked the wrong movies.
