# Strange Flexes I Will Defend Forever

I am immune to shoelace-related accidents.
My shoelaces untie themselves within five minutes of leaving home. I don’t retie them because I’ve accepted my fate. Also I respect free will. 
And yet—I have never tripped. 
Not once. Not because of me. Not because of someone else. At this point, it’s not luck. It’s a superpower.

I once survived 2.5 days on just milk.
No food. Just milk. 
Was I okay? Debatable.
Was this nutritionally sound? Absolutely not.
Did my body send warning signals? Probably but I clearly missed it.

Dodged COVID while being hospitalized during peak COVID.
12 days in Hospital A, followed by 2 days in Hospital B.
One biopsy. A completely wrecked immune system..
COVID skimmed my medical chart and quietly backed off.
As Bollywood would say: “Maut ko chhooke tak se wapas aayi hoon.” (I’ve returned after touching the edge of death.)
Not a flex—but it does feel like one.


There was a time I could solve a 3×3 Rubik’s Cube in 45 seconds.
We oiled the cube so it would spin with one finger.
Was it ethical? Questionable.
Was it effective? Absolutely.


Most of the cool tech I own was free.
Office swag. Conference handouts. Hackathons Goodies. Gift cards.
I don’t purchase fancy things. Fancy things simply find me.

A plant survived me for > 6 months
I have kept a pineapple bromeliad alive for over 6 months.
Given my past with plants, this is less “plant care”
and more “plant hostage situation.”

I’ve watched Friends, BBT, and HIMYM so many times that I’ve lost count.
I don’t “watch” these shows anymore.
I emotionally hover around them.
They play while I make life decisions I’ll regret later.
