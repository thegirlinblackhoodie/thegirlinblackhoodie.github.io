# Misadventures with My Work Bestie

We planned this sweet little “let’s travel together and have each other’s backs” work trip to San Diego. Except the plan made zero sense: I live three miles from San Jose airport, he lives in San Francisco… and somehow I ended up traveling north to SF just so we could fly south together. Why? No one knows. Not even God. But we committed.

We finally reached San Diego and were excited to make the most of our one free afternoon. Our rooms were stacked one above the other on the 1st and 2nd floors, so we both had the same amazing view—a gorgeous golf course that opened right up to the beach. Naturally, we decided to walk down there and enjoy it — until I noticed a giant PRIVATE PROPERTY, TRESPASSERS WILL BE PROSECUTED sign. I stopped immediately. He, on the other hand, was already halfway to felony charges and told me to “just act like we belong.”

We DID NOT belong. Jeans, T-shirts, no golf bag, no confidence—just two confused tourists pretending to be members of a club that would never admit us.

Five minutes in, someone in a golf cart started yelling. I genuinely prepared for my first legal case. But he just warned us that we might get hit by golf balls. Which, honestly, is exactly how the universe would choose to take me out—quietly, stupidly, and in front of witnesses.

And then Monday arrived—fresh day, fresh chaos. Our offices track attendance through badge-ins. He, being an absolute gentleman and sweetheart, opened every single door for me all day—entrances, exits, everything. 

It wasn’t until I got back to the hotel that night that I realized: I HADN'T BADGED EVEN ONCE!!!

Which meant the system thinks I spent the whole day wafting around the building like a ghost.
So even though I was in the office… I owed an extra in-person day.

In short: I flew backwards, trespassed forwards, and still didn’t get credit for showing up. Tragic. Hilarious. Peak me.
