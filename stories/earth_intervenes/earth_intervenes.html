It was around 1:30AM on a weekday. I had work the next morning, but sleep was nowhere in sight. Since I was already awake, I decided—why not make it productive? Or at least feel productive. So I chose to take a head bath and try out a curly hair routine.


Now, I have a habit. When I take a bath, I really take a bath. Long, slow, standing-under-the-hot-water-like-it’s-a-life-reset kind of bath. So much so that there have been times when the continuous hot water has made my BP drop, leaving me dizzy. I’ve learned my lesson (sort of), so now I keep a bottle of cold water nearby—just in case I need to recover quickly.


Anyway, there I was, fully immersed in the moment. Standing under the shower, head down, staring at the bathtub floor, lost in my thoughts. Wondering what I was doing with my life. Thinking about how far I’d come… and yet somehow feeling like I was nowhere close to anything meaningful.


A bit too deep for 1:30 AM? Maybe. Just on the edge of spiraling into darker thoughts.


And then suddenly—it hit.


For the first two seconds:
Oh god, I’m feeling dizzy. Too much hot water. I think I’m going to faint.


I placed my hands against the wall and the sliding shower door on either side, trying to steady myself. It didn’t help.


Next two seconds:
This is it. I’m actually going to faint here. Oh god—what if I faint and the water keeps falling on me and I die? Is this how it ends? I will be found… naked… in a bathtub… by whoever, whenever they find me. No. Absolutely not. That is NOT how I want my life to end.


And then, the next two seconds:
Wait… I feel fine? Pause. What just happened…?


And then it clicked. OH GOD. It was an earthquake!!!


I calmly stepped out of the shower, dried myself, got dressed… and went to bed peacefully.


I guess my 1:30 AM thoughts were so intense, the Earth decided to intervene and shake me out of them.
